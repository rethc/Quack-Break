# Quack Break privacy policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Quack Break is an app that reminds you to rest your eyes. This page explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## The short version

Quack Break does not collect, send or share any personal information. Everything stays on your phone.

## What the app keeps on your phone

To work, the app saves a few things on your device:

- your settings, such as how often to remind you, quiet hours and sounds
- when your next break is due
- how many breaks you took each day
- your pond progress and your duck's name

This information never leaves your phone. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is not included in your phone's cloud backup. If you uninstall the app, it is deleted.

## Permissions the app uses

- **Notifications**, to remind you when it's time for a break.
- **Alarms and reminders**, so reminders arrive on time. You can turn this off in your phone's settings.
- **Run at startup**, so reminders keep working after your phone restarts.
- **Vibration**, to vibrate with a reminder if you choose.

## No accounts, ads or tracking

There is no sign-up and no account. The app shows no ads and contains no analytics or tracking tools. It does not need an internet connection.

## Children

Quack Break is not made for children under 13. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.
